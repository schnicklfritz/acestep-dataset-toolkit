=== Band Profile: The Doors ===
Genres: Psychedelic rock, garage rock, blues rock, acid rock.
Vocals: Smooth-to-aggressive male baritone vocals, spoken word poetry, powerful belted delivery, dark charismatic delivery, rebellious mood.
Key Instruments: Vox Continental organ, Gibson G-101 combo organ, fingerpicked Gibson SG Special electric guitar with P-90 pickups, Fender Rhodes piano bass, Baldwin electric harpsichord, Ludwig acoustic drum kit with driving bossa nova rhythms.
Production Style: 1960s analog production, Sunset Sound studio room ambiance, vintage tube mic saturation, plate reverb, raw unpolished live energy.
