Important Note on Usage

According to the official guidelines, these tags serve two distinct purposes:

    Caption (Global Image): Used for defining the overall genre, mood, instruments, and vocal style.

    Lyrics (Time Script): Used for structuring the song with section markers and controlling specific vocal deliveries at precise moments.

The model is designed to understand and combine these tags to generate complex musical compositions.
